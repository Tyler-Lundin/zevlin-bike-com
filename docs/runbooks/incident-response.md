# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels

- SEV1: Active customer impact or data/security compromise
- SEV2: Major feature outage with workaround
- SEV3: Limited-impact defect

## Immediate actions

1. Open incident channel and appoint incident commander.
2. Capture timeline and evidence from logs/alerts.
3. Contain blast radius (disable endpoint/rotate key/restrict access).
4. Communicate status every 30 minutes for SEV1.
5. Validate recovery with regression checks.
6. Publish postmortem within 3 business days.
